# OVERCLOCK

**[▶ Play the arcade](https://gucciko82-sys.github.io/overclock-arcade/)**

Fifty-three games and one school, all free, all in the browser. Nothing to
install, no account, no adverts, and nothing is ever collected about you or
your children.

---

## What's here

**The arcade** — action, puzzle and sports games. Brick breakers, twin-stick
shooters, tetromino wells, pool, darts, bowling, chess, draughts, solitaire,
a hunting game, a pinball table, and a good many more.

**THE FORGE** — a school that starts a child wherever they actually are and
works upward, at their pace, for years. 192 skills across twelve subjects,
from grade 3 to grade 12: number, fractions, measure, data, algebra, words,
grammar, punctuation, word roots, science, reading and writing.

It never asks a child what level they are. It works that out by watching, and
it moves them up only when they have genuinely earned it — and back down,
quickly and without fuss, if a topic turns out to be too much for now.

On every miss it explains **why**. Not "wrong" — the actual rule that broke,
then the worked solution one line at a time, with a reason on each line. There
is no red, no buzzer and no "incorrect" anywhere in it. It also keeps a record
of what a child has learned, draws it as a chart a grown-up can read in five
seconds, and exports the lot to a spreadsheet.

---

## Your privacy, plainly

**Nothing leaves your device. Ever.**

Every game here is a single HTML file with no server behind it, no analytics,
no adverts, no trackers and no accounts. There is no code in any of these
games that is capable of sending your data anywhere, because there is no
network code in them at all.

A child's progress in THE FORGE is saved in your own browser, on your own
machine, and it stays there. Nobody else can see it — not us, not anyone.
If you want a copy, there is a button that exports it to a spreadsheet.
If you want it gone, clearing your browser data is enough.

The program is identical for everyone. It adapts to the child, on the child's
own computer.

---

## How to use it

Click the link, pick a game, play. It works on a phone, a tablet, a laptop or
a desktop, in either orientation, with touch, keyboard or a game controller.

To keep it on a phone: open the link, then "Add to Home Screen". It will
launch like an app and works offline afterwards.

There is nothing to buy and nothing to sign up for.
